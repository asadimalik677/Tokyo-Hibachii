About Tokyo Hibachi

A polished Japanese and Asian restaurant built around flavor, heat, and atmosphere.

Tokyo Hibachi serves Ithaca with a premium hibachi dining room feel: dramatic grill energy, generous plates, fresh rolls, and a warm red-black-gold atmosphere that feels right for dinner dates, family nights, and celebrations.

Located at 722 S Meadow St, Ithaca, NY 14850, the restaurant welcomes local guests, Cornell and Ithaca College students, families, and visitors across the Finger Lakes area.
