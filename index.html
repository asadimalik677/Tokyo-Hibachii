About Us

Premium Japanese flavor with a lively hibachi spirit.

Tokyo Hibachi brings Japanese, hibachi, and Asian restaurant favorites to Ithaca, NY with a polished dining experience for date nights, family dinners, student gatherings, and celebrations.

From the glow of the grill to carefully plated rolls and comforting noodle dishes, every visit is designed to feel warm, flavorful, and memorable.
